# BeatVibe Studio — "One Drop" · channel trailer · Watercolor Brush

45 s · 1920×1080 · English narration and burned-in subtitles · original score

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **A day in sound (journal kept over one day).** Three locked journal pages, morning focus → evening drive → night sleep, each painted over the last. Clear, but three equal pages read as a list and the channel's range (energy ↔ peace) never meets in one picture.
2. **Anatomy of a beat (specimen plate).** One painted pair of headphones dissected into labelled parts: kick, snare, melody, "vibe". Clever for a producer audience, but it talks only to the beat-maker half of the channel and leaves out the healing and meditation half, which is now most of its uploads.
3. **One drop, one line, two worlds (handscroll transect).** A single drop of pigment hits blank paper on the first kick and becomes a painted sound-wave line. The camera follows that one line to the right: it turns into a night skyline that bursts alight on the drop (beats, hip-hop, EDM); the music stops, mist washes the city away; the same line turns into the still horizon of a mountain lake with a lotus (chill, meditation, healing). We pull back and see both worlds were one sheet, joined by the line, and the channel's name is painted under it.

**Chosen: 3.** It is the only one that puts the whole channel (energy *and* peace) on one continuous sheet, and its turn (a loud world falls silent and becomes a calm one) is the channel's actual range. The line is the channel's own idea ("let the vibes of music connect us") made literal: one line connects the two worlds.

Opening image: a vermilion drop landing on blank paper on the first beat (not a logo). Ending: the pull-back to the whole sheet, the title painted, and one last drop landing on the seal, echoing the first. Score shape: one kalimba note → a hip-hop groove that peaks → true silence → singing bowl and soft pad → the opening motif returns over a gentle pulse.

## 2. Logline and arc

A single drop of sound becomes a line that runs through a city that never sleeps and a lake that never stirs — every vibe, one channel.

- **Setup** (0–5 s): drop → line. "Every beat starts with a single drop."
- **Rise** (5–19 s): the line becomes a skyline; the groove builds; on the bar-5 downbeat the whole city lights at once.
- **Turn** (19–21 s): music cut, brush lifted, mist erases the city to paper. Silence.
- **Calm** (21–35 s): first sound after the silence is a singing bowl; the line is now the horizon of a still lake; a lotus paints itself in.
- **Ending** (35–45 s): pull back, both worlds on one sheet joined by the line; "BeatVibe Studio" painted; the seal; the last drop.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Naturalist field-journal plates and long handscroll panoramas** (grammar of the style itself), and **title sequences of music documentaries that visualise sound as a drawn line** (the waveform-as-landscape idea).
- **Learn:** one continuous camera path over one world; the line as the subject that changes meaning; quantised palette bands that shift with mood; a real silence before the turn.
- **Don't take:** any specific artwork, any recognisable music-brand imagery, logos, melodies, or a "visualiser" look (bars, neon equaliser). Nothing vector; everything stays brush strokes on paper.

## 6. Cue map

- Tempo **90 BPM**, 4/4: beat = 0.667 s, bar = 2.667 s. Bars start at 0, 2.67, 5.33, 8.0, 10.67, 13.33, 16.0, 18.67, 21.33, 24.0, 26.67, 29.33, 32.0, 34.67, 37.33, 40.0, 42.67.
- **A · Drop** (bars 1–2, 0–5.33): kalimba motif (D minor pentatonic: A–C–D…), soft felt piano pad chord Dm9.
- **B · Groove** (bars 3–5, 5.33–13.33): hip-hop kit (kick on 1 & 3+, clap on 2 & 4, soft hats), kalimba motif loops, guitar plucks.
- **C · Lights** (bars 6–7, 13.33–18.67): add sub-bass with harmonics and a warm pad; fullest point. Burst on 13.33 exactly.
- **Silence** (bar 8, 18.67–21.33): music out. Only paper bed and the mist's rustle.
- **D · Calm** (bars 9–13, 21.33–34.67): singing bowl at 21.33, then soft pad (Fmaj7 → Dm9), slow kalimba at half density, no drums.
- **E · Resolve** (bars 14–17, 34.67–45): opening motif returns over a gentle kick on 1 only, pad; seal on 42.67; last kalimba note with the drop at 44.0, ring-out.

Every visual hit (drop 0.67, burst 13.33, music cut 18.67, bowl 21.33, seal 42.67, last drop 44.0) is on a beat of this grid, and read from one timeline in `timeline.json`.
